# BrumSchtick Constitution 🧱⚡🤝

This document defines what BrumSchtick is, what it is not, and the principles that guide decisions.

## What BrumSchtick Is ✅

BrumSchtick is a forever-evolving level design platform intended to become the de facto answer for classic idTech (1-4) mapping needs.

Its core promise is simple:

- Make level design quicker.
- Make level design easier.
- Make level design more enjoyable.

## What BrumSchtick Is Not ❌

BrumSchtick is not a slow, traditionally developed project.

It intentionally favors momentum, practical delivery, and fast iteration over legacy process gravity.

## The Ten Commandments 📜🔥

| # | Commandment | Commitment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `UNIFICATION` | Unite the split mapping community, especially Radiant and TrenchBroom users, by supporting versatile interaction styles and workflows. |
| 2 | `UTILITY` | Continuously increase practical capabilities in many directions so users can do more inside one toolchain. |
| 3 | `EASE-OF-USE` | Keep reducing friction so level design becomes more approachable, less error-prone, and faster to learn. |
| 4 | `GAME SUPPORT` | Keep expanding support for more games and mods, with a bias toward real creator demand. |
| 5 | `MODERNISATION` | Stay current with modern expectations in UX, tooling, distribution, and platform standards. |
| 6 | `AGENTIC` | Use the latest beneficial agentic models/methods to streamline development and bug resolution. |
| 7 | `INNOVATIVE` | Push beyond expected boundaries for what a level design tool can do. |
| 8 | `SEAMLESS` | Blur boundaries across the development platform so workflows feel connected instead of fragmented. |
| 9 | `COMPATIBLE` | Maintain cross-platform support (`Windows`, `macOS`, `Linux`) and expand further when worthwhile opportunities appear. |
| 10 | `COMMUNAL` | Actively invite community contribution, feedback, and co-ownership of direction. |

## Decision Filter For Contributors and Agents 🧠🛠️

When proposing or implementing changes:

- Tie each significant next step to one or more commandments.
- Prefer options that increase unification, utility, and ease-of-use together.
- Treat compatibility and community impact as first-class constraints, not afterthoughts.
- Choose modern, agent-assisted acceleration when it improves outcomes without reducing quality.
